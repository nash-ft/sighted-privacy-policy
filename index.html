Privacy Policy for Sighted

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Sighted ("the app," "we," "us") helps users report and receive alerts about wildlife sightings near them. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Location data. When you report a sighting, the app records your device's GPS location at the time of the report, so it can be shown to other users on the map. When you save an area (like a trailhead or your home), the app stores that location so it can match future sightings against it. Location data is only collected when you actively use these features — we do not track your location continuously in the background.

Sighting reports. Information you submit about a sighting — animal type, color/description, and direction — is stored and shown to other users of the app. Sighting reports automatically expire and stop being shown after 24 hours.

Device identifier. The app generates a random identifier stored on your device, used to associate your saved areas, sighting reports, and moderation actions (reporting or blocking) with your device. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any other personally identifying information, and it is not shared with advertisers.

Push notification token. If you enable notifications, the app registers your device with Apple's push notification service (via Expo's push service) so it can alert you about sightings near your saved areas. This token is stored and used only for sending you these alerts.

How We Use Information

We use the information above solely to operate the app's core features: showing sightings on a map, alerting you to sightings near your saved areas, and letting you flag or block problematic content.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or share it with data brokers.

Third-Party Services

Sighted uses the following third-party service providers to operate:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and backend infrastructure.
  • Expo, which provides push notification delivery.

These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by their own privacy and security practices.

Data Retention

  • Sighting reports are automatically removed from active display after 24 hours; the underlying record may be retained briefly afterward for moderation and abuse-prevention purposes before deletion.
  • Saved areas, your device identifier, and your push token persist until you delete the app or manually remove a saved area.

Your Choices

  • You can deny or later revoke location and notification permissions in your device settings; some features (reporting a sighting, receiving alerts) will not work without them.
  • You can delete a saved area at any time within the app.
  • You can block another reporter's device to stop seeing their sightings.
  • To request deletion of your data, contact us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

Sighted is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
